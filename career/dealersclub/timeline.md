# Career timeline — Werlesson Vieira @ Cars2You / Dealersclub

Derived from monthly commit distribution across all repositories. Narrative labels are **(inferred)** from module activity and commit messages.

---

## 2022 — Platform buildout (highest-volume year)
- **Joined and became the primary front-end engineer** on the Dealersclub ERP (`front`) and the partner app (`thirdparty`), both starting April 2022.
- Front: 330 commits — heavy buildout Jul–Dec (45–55 commits/month sustained).
- Delivered core operational modules: dispatch/despacho, advisory removal ("remoção de assessoria"), event/vehicle logic, courtyard.
- Launched the public `showroom` (Nuxt + Tailwind, SSR/SEO) — initial scaffold.
- Stack: Nuxt 2 / Vue 2 / TypeScript / Vuetify.

## 2023 — Feature expansion & consolidation
- Front: 253 commits; thirdparty: 60 commits (its busiest year).
- Broadened across commercial, financial and inspection domains; expanded partner-facing conditional-report and inspection flows.
- Continued as top contributor on both front-end apps.

## 2024 — Modernization & structural work
- Front: 96 commits, including a **large middleware/routing refactor** (single commit ~25k lines, May 2024).
- Added `portfolio_id` / Principal-model features and cost-per-vehicle logic in `thirdparty`.
- Showroom: added electric-vehicle listing (Feb 2024).
- Shift from pure feature delivery toward refactoring and platform maintenance.

## 2025 — Full-stack expansion into the backend
- **Started backend development on the Laravel 10 `api`** (April 2025): invoices, PDF/QR document generation, Excel import/export, client email flows, OpenAI/Sentry integrations.
- Front/thirdparty move to targeted maintenance (25 / 2 commits).
- Read-only financial view ("financeiro somente leitura") delivered (Sep 2025, ~6.6k lines).

## 2026 — Cross-stack maintenance & polish
- API: invoice contact-info & value-formatting fixes (through May 2026 — most recent commit).
- Front: operational-cards and filtering enhancements (Mar 2026).
- Thirdparty: advisory dashboard refactor & layout cleanup (Mar 2026).
- Now operating **full-stack** across Vue front-ends and the Laravel API.

---

### Trajectory summary
**Front-end specialist (2022–2024) → refactoring/modernization (2024) → full-stack engineer (2025–2026).** Four years of continuous contribution across 4 applications and ~15 business domains, with a clear progression from feature buildout to architecture-level work and backend ownership.
